FAQ

Is this therapy?

No. This program is educational life-coaching content focused on planning, goals, habits and accountability. It is not medical or mental-health treatment and does not replace licensed professional care.

Will this guarantee a specific result?

No. The course provides frameworks and exercises, but results depend on each person's circumstances and actions. There are no income, career or life-outcome guarantees.

How do I get access after paying?

Keep your Cash App payment confirmation. During the founding launch, paid access is verified and delivered manually so the paid files are not exposed publicly on GitHub Pages.

What is the “Secret Sauce”?

The Secret Sauce is the AI Execution system: practical workflows for using AI to turn goals, messy thoughts and obstacles into clearer decisions and realistic next actions while keeping your own judgment in charge. The dedicated Secret Sauce Workbook is a paid resource and is delivered privately after payment verification.

What is different about the $500 option?

The Advanced option includes the Core program plus a deeper workbook with planning, decision, AI-prompt, weekly-review, reset, and 30/60/90-day systems.

Do I need to give you my email or phone number for the free workbook?

No. The free workbook can be downloaded directly from this website. Text updates are completely optional.

Can I start with the free training?

Yes. The Reinvention Blueprint is intended to stand on its own and give you a useful starting framework before you decide whether the full program is right for you.
