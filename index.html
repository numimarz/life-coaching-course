FAQ

Is this therapy?

No. This program is educational life-coaching content focused on planning, goals, habits and accountability. It is not medical or mental-health treatment and does not replace licensed professional care.

Will this guarantee a specific result?

No. The course provides frameworks and exercises, but results depend on each person's circumstances and actions. There are no income, career or life-outcome guarantees.

How do I get access after paying?

During the initial manual-launch version, payment is verified manually and course access is sent to the buyer using the contact instructions provided at checkout.

Can I start with the free training?

Yes. The Reinvention Blueprint is intended to stand on its own and give you a useful starting framework before you decide whether the full program is right for you.
